CLIO — AI advertising platform (team lead)

University capstone led with a three-person team: an integrated advertising concept that explored generative text-to-video, competitor ad research, performance analytics, and hybrid collaborative filtering — built under severe compute constraints and presented at the COMSATS exhibition.

Problem

Turning written campaign concepts into video ads usually needs production teams and budget. CLIO asked whether generative AI, recommendation, and analytics could sit in one advertising workflow — without pretending we had enterprise GPU infrastructure.

Motivation

Named for storytelling and Greek mythology, CLIO treated advertisements as narrative artifacts: generate from text, explore competitors, measure performance, and recommend creatives with hybrid collaborative filtering (including synthetic data for recommender experimentation).

Approach

  • Team of three — I led integration and ownership across modules
  • Auth, analytics, competitor exploration, and recommender design
  • Text-to-video pipeline experiments with LoRA fine-tuning of CogVideoX-5B
  • ~200 manually curated and hand-captioned videos; limited Colab A100 sessions
  • Final fine-tuning ran during the 2025 India–Pakistan conflict — amid disruptions, with a teammate, on whatever GPU time we could still reach
  • Exhibition demo at COMSATS University Islamabad

Status (honest)

  • Not a fully deployed or currently operational platform
  • Text-to-video engine is not hosted today — GPU / compute constraints (no sponsored infra; only short Colab A100 windows) made sustained hosting unrealistic
  • Frontend and integrated modules were built for the academic project, not kept live
  • Experimental generation results were obtained under those limits; the full stack was never a production product
  • The recommendation system remains the independently accessible component

Architecture (intended system)

Auth / login → Text → video experiments → Competitor ad explore → Performance analytics → Hybrid CF recommender

What this demonstrates

Team leadership under constraint, generative AI experimentation, recommender-system thinking, and the difference between a research demo and a shipped product.

Technology

Python / ML experimentation (CogVideoX-5B + LoRA), collaborative filtering (ALS-style ranking in the accessible recommender), Flask API, Next.js UI for the recommendation surface, synthetic data for recommender training loops.
