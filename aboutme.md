## 2. University Capstone — CLIO

CLIO is one of my most significant academic projects and should receive particular attention.

I led a team of three people in developing an integrated AI-powered advertising platform.

The project was inspired by storytelling and Greek mythology, exploring how generative AI could transform written concepts into video advertisements.

The envisioned platform incorporated:
- User authentication and login.
- Text-to-video advertisement generation.
- Competitor advertisement exploration.
- Advertising performance analytics.
- A recommendation engine using hybrid collaborative filtering.
- Synthetic data for recommendation-system experimentation and training.

### Technical challenges and experimentation

The most challenging component was the text-to-video generation pipeline.

We lacked external sponsorship and dedicated computational infrastructure. To work within these limitations:

- Explored LoRA fine-tuning of CogVideoX-5B.
- Curated a dataset of 200 manually selected videos.
- Hand-captioned the videos for experimentation.
- Used limited Google Colab A100 GPU sessions.
- Worked around constrained GPU availability and compute time.
- Successfully obtained experimental video-generation results.

Our final experimentation took place during the 2025 India-Pakistan conflict, amid significant disruptions and the sound of military aircraft. I continued working alongside a friend and teammate during an especially demanding final fine-tuning session.

We ultimately presented CLIO at the COMSATS University project exhibition.

### Important status clarification

Do not portray CLIO as a fully deployed or currently operational platform.

The project was developed and demonstrated academically, but computational limitations prevented us from hosting the complete integrated platform.

The current situation:
- The recommendation system is the component that remains independently accessible.
- The text-to-video engine is not currently hosted.
- The frontend and other integrated modules were developed during the project but are not currently deployed.
- Experimental generation results were obtained, but the complete system was not delivered as a live production product.

Present this distinction accurately.

The project demonstrates team leadership, experimental ML, resourcefulness, generative AI research and system-level thinking.
