Craft

Not a checklist — a way of working.

Tools matter. Judgment matters more. Here’s how I move from a messy question to something you can trust, reproduce, and ship.

  1. 01

    Machine learning & modelling

    I start with the decision the model must support — then pick the simplest approach that earns its complexity. Trees, tabular deep learning, and classical baselines sit side by side until the data picks a winner.

    In rotation PyTorch · Scikit-learn · XGBoost · TabNet · EBM · feature engineering

  2. 02

    Statistical thinking & experiments

    Lift isn’t a vibe. I design A/B setups, read confidence intervals and effect sizes, and write recommendations that say ship, iterate, or stop — with the uncertainty still visible.

    In rotation A/B testing · bootstrap CIs · hypothesis testing · experiment design · SQL

  3. 03

    AI systems & generative pipelines

    LLMs and retrieval only help when answers stay grounded. I build RAG flows, recommendation loops, and generative experiments with clear limits on what the system is allowed to claim.

    In rotation RAG · FAISS · Sentence-Transformers · Groq · LoRA / CogVideoX experiments · hybrid collaborative filtering

  4. 04

    Systems that can be audited

    Notebooks become APIs. Provenance gets labeled. Policies ship with evidence. I care about MLflow trails, Dockerised services, and interfaces people can actually operate.

    In rotation Python · FastAPI · Flask · MLflow · Docker · Next.js · Streamlit · Plotly

  5. 05

    Business & growth analytics

    At Digitalis I’ve lived on the commercial side of data — ads, CRM, audits, competitors, revenue questions. Numbers are interesting; what they change about a decision is the point.

    In rotation Google Analytics · Meta Ads · CRM analysis · competitor research · performance reporting

I optimise for

  • Rigorous experiments
  • Reproducibility
  • Honest model limits
  • Resource-efficient builds
  • Research → practical systems
